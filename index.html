About

I am a third-year undergraduate student majoring in Biomedical Engineering at South China University of Technology . Currently, I am working as an algorithm intern at Omni Intelligence , where I focus on advancing algorithmic research in BCI Foundation Models under the supervision of Dr. Kexin Lou and Dr. Chen Wei. Previously, I was a visiting student at Southern University of Science and Technology , under the supervision of Prof. Quanying Liu , where I focused on parameter-efficient personalized adaptation and test-time adaptation methods for EEG foundation models. I also worked under the supervision of Prof. Yanwu Xu and Prof. Lin Shu .

My primary research interests include BCI Foundation Models , Human-AI Alignment , Medical AI , and Human-Agent Interaction , especially in LLMs and agentic applications. Always eager to expand my research experience, I am seeking opportunities for research internships in 2026 and Ph.D. positions in 2027. If you are interested in chatting with me, feel free to drop me an email .
